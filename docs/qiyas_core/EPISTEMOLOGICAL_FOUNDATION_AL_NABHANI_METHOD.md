## 1. أصل المنهج عند النبهاني - Al-Nabhani's Foundational Method

### 1.1 The Four Elements of Thinking

Al-Nabhani establishes that **thinking cannot occur** without four elements:

1. **واقع** (wāqi') - Reality or its trace
2. **دماغ صالح** (dimāgh ṣāliḥ) - Sound brain/processor
3. **حس** (ḥiss) - Sensation/perception
4. **معلومات سابقة** (ma'lūmāt sābiqah) - Prior information

### 1.2 Definition of Rational Thought (العقل/الفكر)

**Al-Nabhani's definition**:

```
العقل = نقل الحس بالواقع بواسطة الحواس إلى الدماغ
       مع وجود معلومات سابقة يفسر بها هذا الواقع
```

**Translation**: Rational thought is the transfer of sensation of reality via the senses to the brain, with prior information by which this reality is interpreted.

### 1.3 The Rational Method as Foundation

Al-Nabhani establishes:

1. **الطريقة العقلية** (al-ṭarīqah al-'aqliyyah) - The rational method is the **foundation**
2. **الطريقة العلمية** (al-ṭarīqah al-'ilmiyyah) - The scientific method is a **branch** or specialized technique
3. The scientific method works **only** on sensible, experimentable matter
4. The scientific method **cannot** be the general foundation for all thinking

### 1.4 Foundational Constraints

```
لا تفكير بلا واقع أو أثر واقع
لا أثر بلا حس أو ما يقوم مقامه
لا فهم للأثر بلا معلومات سابقة
لا استعمال للمعلومات السابقة بلا مجال
لا نتيجة بلا ربط مضبوط بين الأثر والمعلومات داخل المجال
```

**Translation**:
- No thinking without reality or trace of reality
- No trace without sensation or its functional equivalent
- No understanding of trace without prior information
- No use of prior information without a domain
- No result without controlled linking of trace and information within the domain

## 2. التعريف الأعلى للتفكير - The Supreme Definition of Thinking

Building on al-Nabhani's foundation, we define thinking as:

```
التفكير = انتقال معرفي محكوم
```

8. While preserving residuals and trace

**Definition**: That something is within a genus or domain.

**Examples**:

- This quantity is within the quantitative domain
- This text is within the discursive domain

**Function**: Opening the correct domain (فتح المجال الصحيح)

**Definition**: That from establishing something, a question, constraint, or obstacle opens.

- If this is a licensed quantitative measure, it can be measured
- If this is merely a trace, it cannot be made into a ruling
- If this is a compressed number, it cannot be made into an authoritative candidate

**Function**: Controlling what follows and what does not follow (ضبط ما يلزم وما لا يلزم)

### 6.4 Critical Principle

```
اللزوم شرط في الانتقال لا موجب مستقل للحكم
```

**Translation**: Entailment is a condition for transition, not an independent cause for ruling.

**Meaning**: That something follows from something is insufficient for accepting the result until it passes through domain, identity, evidence, obstacle, and rank.
